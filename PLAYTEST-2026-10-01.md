## Corrected along the way

- The first notorious filter was too broad: "criminal" and "convicted" caught
  a criminal defence lawyer, the nurse who helped stop a serial killer, and
  fraudsters. Narrowed to violent and sexual crimes and their victims.
- Correcting a Wikipedia clue's "born YYYY" from the stored birth date was
  tried and reversed: where the two disagreed, the stored date was usually the
  wrong one (Anne Hathaway "1556" was Shakespeare's wife).
